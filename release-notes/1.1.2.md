# 165 Armer 1.1.2

## What's changed since 1.1.1

- **Camera at 120 Hz** (issue #28). With the phone on **Standard**, the
  camera's buttons and menus dropped to 60 Hz. The lock-screen switch in
  Settings now covers the camera as well, and is renamed **Lock screen and
  camera at 120 Hz**. While the camera is open the screen is held at 120 Hz,
  and the hold is released when you leave it. The camera half needs
  **Usage access** (the row at the top of Settings) to see the camera.

**Upgrading:** signed with the same key as 1.1.1, so it installs over it and
keeps your armed apps and settings. If the lock-screen switch was on, the
camera is covered straight away.
