# 165 Armer 1.1.3

## What's changed since 1.1.2

- **Screen-off animation at 120 Hz** (issue #31). With **Lock screen and
  camera at 120 Hz** on, the animation of the screen going off (most visible
  as the clock moving into the Always-On display) still ran at 60 Hz. The
  120 Hz hold only began once the screen was fully off. It now begins as the
  screen starts going off.

**Upgrading:** signed with the same key as 1.1.2, so it installs over it and
keeps your armed apps and settings.
