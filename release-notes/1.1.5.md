# 165 Armer 1.1.5

## What's changed since 1.1.4

- **The app list refreshes by itself** (issue #36). Install or remove an app
  while 165 Armer is open and the list follows, with no restart.
- **Arm all includes apps you install later** (issue #36), at the same rate,
  until you press Clear.
- **Starting after a reboot** (issue #41). The watchdog now starts before the
  saved apps are re-armed, not after, and restarts after an update. On
  ColorOS it still needs Auto-launch allowed for the app.
- **Idle ramp for 165 Hz** (issue #37). A new switch in Settings, off by
  default, for screens that stay at 165 Hz when nothing needs it (OnePlus
  15R, Ace 6T). A quiet screen gets the 120 Hz vote, so the phone can ramp
  down by itself: to its idle rate on a still screen, and to 30 or 60 Hz
  while video plays. Experimental: leave it off on the OnePlus 15 and 16.
- **Video no longer ends the idle ramp.** A 144 Hz app that had gone quiet
  went back to 144 Hz when a 60 fps video started. It now stays in the 120 Hz
  vote, where video settles at 30 or 60 Hz, until something needs more than
  120.
- **Faster rate on app switch** (issues #39, #40). An armed app gets its rate
  as soon as it comes to the front, not up to five seconds later.
- **Better logs for the lock screen at 120 Hz** (issue #42).

**Upgrading:** signed with the same key as 1.1.4, so it installs over it and
keeps your armed apps and settings.
